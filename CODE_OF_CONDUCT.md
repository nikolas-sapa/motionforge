# Code of Conduct

MotionForge welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Disagreement about ideas and code is fine; personal attacks are not.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, retaliation, spam, and sharing credentials or private video and capture data.

Maintainers will respond in good faith and may take proportionate action, including a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.

## Reporting

For private or sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer through an available channel on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/nikolas-sapa). If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting a private contact method. Do not include personal or sensitive details in a public issue.
